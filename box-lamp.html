Box Lamp

Sustainable Light Bulb packaging that unfolds into a lamp. Only cardboard and plastic bag waste were used in construction. LED light bulbs give off minimal heat, so paper-based materials can be used with them in the home. The cord coils into a separate compartment at the top of the box for added storability. The lamp can be changed easily by pushing the bulb out of the side openings in the plastic. The box lamp can be displayed hanging by its cord, or sitting on its base.

The Lamp opens by pulling the cord up from the top, collapsing the sides. The plastic lampshade follows the collapsing box, stretching until tight.
