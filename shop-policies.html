Shipping & Returns

Last updated September 30, 2026

Made to order

Each piece is 3D printed after you order it and usually ships within 5–7 business days. Shipping is free within the United States. Orders currently ship to US addresses only.

Damaged or misprinted items

If your order arrives broken or misprinted, email a photo within 30 days of delivery and I’ll send a free replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer. You don’t need to send the damaged item back.

Change of mind

Because every piece is made to order, I can’t accept returns or refunds for a change of mind.

Cancellations

Email me as soon as possible if you need to cancel. If your order hasn’t started printing yet, I’ll cancel it and refund you in full.

Contact

Email noahjtaylor42@gmail.com with any questions about an order. I usually reply within two business days.
